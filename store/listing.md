## English (United States) — en-US

**App name**
```
Road Rush 3D
```

**Short description**
```
Hop past cars, logs and trains and take the chicken as far as you can!
```

**Full description**
```
Cross the city one hop at a time! In Road Rush 3D you help a brave chicken (and its friends) cross busy roads, rivers full of logs and train tracks — without getting hit and without falling behind.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the screen to hop forward.
• Swipe sideways or back to dodge.
• Watch out for cars, trucks and trains.
• On rivers, jump onto logs and lily pads.
• Don't stand still: the cloud is coming and swallows anyone left behind!

HIGHLIGHTS
• Colorful, cute 3D graphics with a panoramic camera.
• Endless world: every game is different.
• Speeds up little by little, without getting tiring.
• 4 animals to unlock with in-game coins: Chicken, Duckling, Piggy and Cat.
• Daily missions and goals with rewards.
• Light and fast, runs well on simple phones.
• Play offline, no sign-up.
• Available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

The game is free and contains ads. Reward videos are always optional: you choose to watch one to continue your game or double your coins. There are no in-app purchases.
```
